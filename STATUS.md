# Delivery status · 17 November 2026

The repository contains the public waiting list, teacher atelier, learner web area, native Swift/Java clients, a Go API, workers, PostgreSQL migrations and S3-backed documents. The local product can be exercised using real email capture, two accounts and separately validated teaching content. Production publication, external account configuration and store distribution remain open.

## Product available locally

- **Public.** Double opt-in waiting list with unsubscribe, a passwordless login and legal templates to complete. Sales default to disabled. The public subject feed requires explicitly authored, validated publication material.
- **Teacher.** The atelier manages chapters, lessons, exercises, mocks and oral prompts in draft or validated status. It supports Markdown/KaTeX, source metadata and PDFs, video URLs and descriptions, CSV/Markdown imports, reference solutions, tolerances and rubrics. Teachers review submitted copies and oral sessions, grade written mock responses individually and inspect correction usage and estimated costs. The atelier also manages Suivi appointments, private WhatsApp invitations and individualized teacher video corrections.
- **Learner web.** Chapter course, teacher-validated exercises, deterministic grading, diagnostic and weekly revision plan, copy submission and review, timed exams and oral sessions. Copy tracking reads the current stage and attempt count from the backend. The browser refreshes active submissions while the page is visible. Mixed mock exams distinguish automatically awarded points, written answers awaiting review and the final grade. The authenticated access matrix drives visible keyboard-accessible locked features and routes them to the matching plan when sales are open.
- **API and jobs.** Revocable shared sessions, role/ownership checks, consent, account export/deletion, student progress, S3 document/photo storage, durable workers and at-least-once job handling. Numeric correction uses code. Uncertain copies can reach the teacher. Text/vision and voice providers require their configured endpoints.
- **Commerce.** The disabled-by-default web flows support configured plans, Checkout, portal, invoices and teacher refunds. Real payment acceptance still needs Stripe test credentials.
- **Mobile.** The iOS source is Swift, Android is Java. Each consumes the common OpenAPI contract and provides learner workflows, including offline practice, synchronization, reminders and image submission. Native subscription flows use StoreKit and Google Play Billing, with Go purchase verification and a membership ledger. Store release builds and real payment acceptance remain separate checks.
